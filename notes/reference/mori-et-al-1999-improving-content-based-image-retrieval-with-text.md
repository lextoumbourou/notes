**Mori et al., 1999** is an early paper on learning from images paired with text: *Image-to-word transformation based on dividing and vector quantizing images with words* by Yasuhide Mori, Hironobu Takahashi and Ryuichi Oka.

They explored improving content-based image retrieval by training a model to predict the nouns and adjectives in text documents paired with images.

It's cited as an early precursor in [Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision](learning-transferable-visual-models-from-natural-language-supervision.md), the CLIP paper.
