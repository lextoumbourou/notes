**Digital Breast Tomosynthesis (DBT)** is a breast imaging technique, often called 3D mammography, where an X-ray tube sweeps in an arc over the compressed breast and takes a series of low-dose images from different angles.

The images are reconstructed into a stack of thin slices that can be scrolled through. This reduces a problem with standard 2D [Mammograms](mammogram.md), where overlapping tissue can hide a lesion or look like one. See [Mammography](mammography.md).
