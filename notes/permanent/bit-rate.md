**Bit Rate** is the number of bits per second required to store or transport audio.

For uncompressed audio, it's the [Sample Rate](sample-rate.md) multiplied by the bit depth and the number of channels. For example, CD audio is 44,100 samples per second × 16 bits × 2 channels = 1,411.2 kbps. Compressed formats like MP3 or AAC get much lower bit rates, like 128 or 256 kbps.
