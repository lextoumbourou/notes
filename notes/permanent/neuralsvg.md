**NeuralSVG** is a text-to-vector generation method that encodes an entire SVG into the weights of a small neural network, instead of optimising the shapes directly. It uses a dropout-based regularisation on shapes to encourage a meaningful, layered structure.

See [NeuralSVG: An Implicit Representation for Text-to-Vector Generation (Jan 2025)](../reference/neuralsvg-an-implicity-represetation-for-text-to-vector-generation-jan-2025.md).
