**Generative Agents** store experiences in natural language, derive higher-level reflections, and retrieve relevant memories to plan behaviour. The paper demonstrates the architecture in a simulated town.

Source: [Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442v2).

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
