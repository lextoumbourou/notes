Graphic representation of data - part of [Descriptive Statistics](descriptive-statistics.md).

Best practices:

* Choose an appropriate type.
* Keep it simple.
* Clearly label axes and provide context.
* Include a clear title and a legend.
* Avoid unnecessary graphics and focus on main message.
* Tailor visualisations to audience needs and understanding.
