**Precision** is a measure of how many results returned are correct.

$\text{Precision} = \frac{TP}{TP + FP}$

where $TP$ is the number of true positives and $FP$ is the number of false positives.

It's usually paired with [Recall](recall.md), and the two are combined in the [F1-Score](f1-score.md). See also [Confusion Matrix](confusion-matrix.md).
