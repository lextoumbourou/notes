**Langevin Dynamics** is a mathematical framework that models the motion of particles under systematic forces and random thermal fluctuations, adding stochastic noise terms to Newton's equations of motion.

It is useful in molecular dynamics simulations and statistical physics, as it can simulate how particles behave in a heat bath or solvent environment, capturing the deterministic forces (like molecular interactions) and the random kick from thermal motion.
