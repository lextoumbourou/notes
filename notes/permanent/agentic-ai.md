**Agentic AI** refers to systems where multiple [AI Agents](ai-agents.md) work together to achieve complex goals, with coordinated communication between agents, planning, breaking tasks into sub-tasks and dynamic allocation of work.

It's distinguished from a single AI Agent, which handles narrow, task-specific automation with limited autonomy. See [AI Agents vs. Agentic AI: A Conceptual Taxonomy, Applications and Challenges](../reference/papers/ai-agents-vs-agentic-ai-a-conceptual-taxonomy-applications-and-challenges.md), and related ideas in [Agentic Reasoning](agentic-reasoning.md) and [Multi-Agent Systems](multi-agent-systems.md).
