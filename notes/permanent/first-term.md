The **First Term** is the starting value of a [Sequence](sequence.md), usually written $ or $.

In an [Arithmetic Sequence](arithmetic-sequence.md), the first term and the [Common Difference](common-difference.md) are enough to define the whole sequence. For example, in 1, 7, 13, 19, ... the first term is 1. The same idea applies to [Geometric Progressions](geometric-progressions.md), together with the common ratio.
