Self-Instruct is a fine-tuning framework for improving the instruction-following capabilities of pretrained language models by bootstrapping off their own generations.

See paper: [Self-Instruct: Aligning Language Models with Self-Generated Instructions](self-instruct-aligning-language-models-with-self-generated-instructions.md).
