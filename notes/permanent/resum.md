**ReSum** periodically condenses interaction history into summaries so an agent can continue longer tasks. The basic method works without training; **ReSum-GRPO** uses reinforcement learning to adapt agents to reasoning over the compressed context.

Source: [ReSum: Unlocking Long-Horizon Search Intelligence via Context Summarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13313v3).

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
