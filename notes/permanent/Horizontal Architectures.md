**Horizontal Architectures** are a type of [Multi-Agent Systems](multi-agent-systems.md) pattern where agents work together as peers, rather than taking direction from a lead agent. All agents can see and contribute to a shared discussion of the task.

They're generally used for tasks where collaboration, feedback and group discussion are key to success. The opposite pattern is a vertical architecture, where a lead agent directs the others and there's a clear division of labour.

The categories come from [The Landscape of Emerging AI Agent Architectures for Reasoning, Planning, and Tool Calling: A Survey](the-landscape-of-emerging-ai-agent-architectures-for-reasoning-planning-and-tool-calling-a-survey.md).

See [Multi-Agent Systems](multi-agent-systems.md).
