**Complete Graph** is a [Simple Graph](simple-graph.md) where every pair of vertices is adjacent (linked by an [Edge](graph-edge.md)).

The complete graph with n vertices is written $. It has these properties:

* Every vertex has degree -1$.
* The sum of the degree sequence is (n-1)$.
* The number of edges is $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$.

A single vertex on its own is also a complete graph. See [Week 13 - Graphs A](../reference/moocs/coursera/uol-discrete-mathematics/week-13-graphs-a.md).
