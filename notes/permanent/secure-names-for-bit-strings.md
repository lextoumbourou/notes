**Secure names for bit-strings** is a 1997 paper by Stuart Haber and W. Scott Stornetta, two of the pioneers of digital timestamping.

It's one of the timestamping papers cited in the Timestamp Server section of [Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System](bitcoin-a-peer-to-peer-electronic-cash-system.md), whose idea of chaining together [Hash Function](hash-function.md) of timestamped data became the basis of the blockchain.
