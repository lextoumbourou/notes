A **binomial random walk** is a type of random walk where each step has a fixed probability of moving in one direction, akin to flipping a biased coin. After a fixed number of steps, the number of steps taken in each direction follows a binomial distribution.

The race between the honest chain and an attacker's chain in [Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System](bitcoin-a-peer-to-peer-electronic-cash-system.md) is modelled as a binomial random walk.
