**Skill-Acquisition Efficiency** is a method of measuring intelligence proposed in [On the Measure of Intelligence (2019)](../reference/papers/on-the-measure-of-intelligence-2019.md), where it's argued that true intelligence lies not in the possession of specific skills, but in the efficiency with which a system can acquire new skills, especially when considering the influence of prior knowledge, experience, and the difficulty of generalising to new tasks. This perspective shifts the focus of AI development from achieving high performance in pre-defined tasks to cultivating broader cognitive abilities that enable adaptability and efficient learning across a range of novel situations.
