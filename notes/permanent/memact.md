**MemAct** (Memory-as-Action) treats working-memory management as learnable actions, including deletion and insertion within the context. It uses reinforcement learning to optimise information retention together with task performance.

Source: [Memory as Action: Autonomous Context Curation for Long-Horizon Agentic Tasks](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.956/).

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
