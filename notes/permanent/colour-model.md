A **Colour Model** is a mathematical way of describing colours as tuples of numbers. For example, [RGB](rgb.md) describes a colour with three numbers (red, green and blue) and CMYK uses four (cyan, magenta, yellow and key/black).

A colour model on its own doesn't say exactly which colour a set of numbers refers to. Pairing a model with a specific mapping to absolute colours gives a [Colour Space](colour-space.md). Device-independent systems from [Colourimetry](colourimetry.md), like [CIE XYZ (1931)](cie-xyz-1931.md) and [CIE Lab](colourspace-cielab.md), are used as the reference for that mapping.
