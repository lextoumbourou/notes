**Adam Back** is a British cryptographer and cypherpunk, best known for inventing [Hashcash](hashcash.md) in 1997, a proof-of-work system for fighting email spam.

Hashcash is cited in [Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System](bitcoin-a-peer-to-peer-electronic-cash-system.md), and it influenced the [Proof-of-Work](proof-of-work.md) mining used by [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md). He's the co-founder and CEO of Blockstream, a Bitcoin technology company.

Some people speculate that he's [Satoshi Nakamoto](satoshi-nakamoto.md), which he has denied.
