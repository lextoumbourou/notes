The **Golden Ratio** is the irrational number $\varphi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618$.

Two quantities $a > b > 0$ are in the golden ratio if the ratio of the larger to the smaller is the same as the ratio of their sum to the larger:

$$
\frac{a + b}{a} = \frac{a}{b} = \varphi
$$

It's the positive solution of $\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1$. The ratio of consecutive Fibonacci numbers ($\frac{8}{5}, \frac{13}{8}, \frac{21}{13}, \ldots$) gets closer and closer to $\varphi$.
