**Sentence Segmentation** is the process of splitting text into individual sentences. It sounds trivial, but full stops aren't always sentence boundaries (think "Dr." or "3.14"), so it usually needs rules or a trained model.

It's a common early step in classical NLP pipelines. See [Text Preprocessing](text-preprocessing.md).
