**Metric Hacking** is when a metric is optimised in a way that improves the number without improving the thing it's meant to measure.

It's a common failure mode of [Proxy Metrics](proxy-metrics.md): metrics can and will be gamed. It's closely related to Goodhart's Law: "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure".

In Kaggle competitions, it refers to tricks that optimise the competition metric in a way that doesn't align with the organiser's goals.

See [Reliance on Metrics is a Fundamental Challenge for AI](../reference/papers/reliance-on-metrics-fundamental-challenge-for-ai.md).
