**Prompt Engineering** is the practice of designing and refining the inputs (prompts) given to [Large Language Models](large-language-models.md) to get better, more reliable outputs.

It includes techniques like giving the model a role, providing examples in the prompt ([In-Context Learning](in-context-learning.md)), and asking it to reason step by step ([Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models](../reference/papers/chain-of-thought-prompting-elicits-reasoning-in-large-language-models.md)).
