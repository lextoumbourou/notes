The Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC) is a dataset of visual reasoning tasks designed to evaluate AI systems' ability to perform general abstract reasoning. It consists of a set of tasks where an AI must infer a pattern from a few examples and apply that pattern to new scenarios, testing the system's capacity for reasoning and generalisation beyond simple pattern matching. See [On the Measure of Intelligence (2019)](../reference/papers/on-the-measure-of-intelligence-2019.md).
