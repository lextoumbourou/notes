A **Scalar** is a single number that only has a magnitude (size), unlike a [Vector](vector.md), which has both a magnitude and a direction.

Distance, speed, mass and temperature are examples of scalar quantities, whereas displacement and velocity are [Vector Quantity](vector-quantity.md).

In linear algebra, multiplying a vector by a scalar stretches or shrinks it, which is why it's called [Vector Scaling](vector-scaling.md).
