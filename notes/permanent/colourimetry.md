Colorimetry is the science and technology used to quantify and describe physical color, including its measurement, representation, and perception. It involves the use of standardized systems, such as the [CIE XYZ (1931)](cie-xyz-1931.md) color space, to ensure consistent color reproduction across different devices and media. By analyzing how colors are perceived by the human eye, colorimetry enables accurate color matching and reproduction in various industries, such as printing, photography, and display manufacturing.
