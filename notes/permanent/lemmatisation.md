**Lemmatisation** is a text preprocessing step that reduces words to their base dictionary form, or lemma. For example, "running" and "ran" both become "run". Unlike stemming, it usually takes the word's context and part of speech into account.

See [Text Preprocessing](text-preprocessing.md).
