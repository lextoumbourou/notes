A **Generative Model** is a model that generates data that has a similar distribution to its training dataset.

In 2024, at the time of writing, some common approaches to generative machine learning are:

1. [Transformer](transformer.md)
2. [Denoising Diffusion Model](denoising-diffusion-model.md)
3. [Generative Adversarial Network](generative-adversarial-network.md)
4. [Variational Auto-Encoder](variational-auto-encoder.md)
