**LLMLingua** compresses prompts using budget allocation and iterative token-level compression. It selects information to retain instead of simply deleting text because it is old.

Source: [LLMLingua: Compressing Prompts for Accelerated Inference of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05736v2).

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
