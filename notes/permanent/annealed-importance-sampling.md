**Annealed Importance Sampling** - Dealing with complex distributions.

This method combines elements of [Importance Sampling](importance-sampling.md) and [Markov Chain Sampling](marvok-chain-sampling.md) to handle distributions with isolated modes, which pose a challenge for traditional methods.
