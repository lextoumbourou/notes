**Ultrasound Imaging** is a medical imaging technique that uses high-frequency [Sound Waves](sound-wave.md) to create images of structures inside the body, based on the echoes that bounce back from tissue. It doesn't use ionising radiation.

In breast cancer detection, it's often used alongside [Mammography](mammography.md), particularly for dense breast tissue, and to tell whether a lump is a fluid-filled cyst or a solid mass.

See also [Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)](magnetic-resonance-imaging-mri.md).
