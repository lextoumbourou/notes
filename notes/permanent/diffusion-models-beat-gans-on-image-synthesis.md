Base page for [Diffusion Models Beat GANs on Image Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.05233) by Prafulla Dhariwal, Alex Nichol.

This OpenAI paper, first released in May 2021, showed that diffusion models could produce better images than GANs, which had been the state of the art for image generation. They got there by improving the model architecture and introducing classifier guidance, which uses a classifier's gradients to steer sampling towards a chosen class, trading some diversity for quality.
