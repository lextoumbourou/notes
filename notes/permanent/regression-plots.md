**Regression Plots** are a [Scatter Plot](scatter-plot.md) with a fitted regression line drawn over the points, usually from a [Linear Regression](linear-regression.md).

They're useful for seeing the strength and direction of the relationship between two variables, and for spotting points that don't fit the trend.

See [Data Visualisation](data-visualisation.md).
