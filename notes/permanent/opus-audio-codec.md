An audio codec described in [Definition of the Opus Audio Codec](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6716). Considered the successor to Ogg Vorbis.

It uses [Linear Prediction](linear-prediction.md) and [Modified Discrete Cosine Transform](modified-discrete-cosine-transform.md) to achieve a strong compression rate.
