A common measure of the spread of data.

$\bar{x}$ is the average of some numbers $x_1, ..., x_n$ 

Standard deviation of those numbers is

$s = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum\limits^{n}_{i=1} (x_i - \bar{x})^2 }$
