## Overview

The research article outlines the development of [AgentVerse](agentverse.md), a novel multi-agent framework that mimics human group problem-solving.

AgentVerse aims to harness the power of large language models (LLMs) by organising them into collaborative teams, which can solve complex problems like text understanding, reasoning, coding and tool utilisation more effectively than a single agent.

The study highlights the emergence of social behaviours within these teams, including volunteerism, conformity, and destructive behaviour.

The article concludes by discussing potential future applications and improvements for AgentVerse, highlighting its potential to revolutionise AI problem-solving.
