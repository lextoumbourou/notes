**GPT** (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) is a [Language Model](language-model.md) architecture from [Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training](../reference/rimproving-language-understanding-by-generative-pre-training.md).

It's a decoder-only [Transformer](transformer.md) that is first pre-trained on a large corpus of unlabelled text to predict the next token, then [Fine-Tuning](fine-tuning.md) on specific downstream tasks. The original model was released by OpenAI in 2018, and the approach was scaled up in later models like GPT-2 and GPT-3.
