**Physical Layer** is the lowest layer of the [TCP/IP Five-Layer Network Model](tcp-ip-5-layer-network-model.md) (and of the [OSI Model](osi-model.md)). It's responsible for transmitting raw bits over a physical medium, like copper cables, fibre optics or radio waves.

It covers things like cables, connectors, voltages and signal timing.

See [TCP/IP Five-Layer Network Model](tcp-ip-5-layer-network-model.md)
