**Nonequilibrium thermodynamics** is about how things naturally flow from high to low - like heat moving from hot to cold regions. Think of it as studying systems actively changing and trying to reach balance rather than systems already at rest.

The math behind [Diffusion Models](diffusion-models.md) is inspired by the actual physical diffusion processes. Like a drop of ink gradually spreads through water following specific patterns, diffusion models start with random noise and gradually "de-noise" it into clear images or data, following similar mathematical principles. The forward process of a diffusion model mirrors physical diffusion, gradually going from an ordered to a disordered state, and the model learns to reverse it, going from disorder back to order. See [Deep Unsupervised Learning Using Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics](../reference/deep-unsupervised-learning-using-nonequilibrium-thermodynamics.md).
