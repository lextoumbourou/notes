**BKG9.8** is a backgammon program built by Hans Berliner at Carnegie Mellon University. In 1979, it beat the newly crowned world champion Luigi Villa 7 points to 1 in an exhibition match, although a lot of its success was attributed to luck.

See [A gamut of games](../reference/articles/a-gamut-of-games.md).
