**Recall** is the proportion of actual positive cases that a model correctly identifies. In other words: of all the correct results, how many did I return?

$\text{Recall} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN}$

It's also known as [Sensitivity](sensitivity.md) or true positive rate. It's usually considered alongside [Precision](precision.md), and the two are combined in the [F1-Score](f1-score.md).
