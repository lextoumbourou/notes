**MemGPT** manages information across memory tiers, moving information between the model's limited context window and external storage. It supports tasks such as long-document analysis and conversations spanning multiple sessions.

Source: [MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560v2).

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
