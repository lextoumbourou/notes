**Hierarchical Generative Model** is a [Generative Model](generative-model.md) that represents data at multiple levels of abstraction. Higher levels capture coarse, global structure and lower levels capture fine detail, with each level conditioned on the one above.

[Generating Diverse High-Fidelity Images with VQ-VAE-2](generating-diverse-high-fidelity-images-with-vq-vae-2.md) is an example: it uses a hierarchy of top and bottom latent codes to generate high-fidelity images.

See also [Hierarchical Model](hierarchical-model.md).
