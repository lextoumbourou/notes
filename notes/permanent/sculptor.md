**Sculptor** gives language models tools for context fragmentation, summarising, hiding and restoring content, and searching. It can work without specific training, and the ICLR 2026 paper also presents reinforcement learning for context-management behaviour.

Source: [Sculptor: Empowering LLMs with Cognitive Agency via Active Context Management](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/f9155a06ba1b20e40c38bf8541db456f-Abstract-Conference.html).
