**Computer-Aided Diagnosis (CADx)** is software that helps clinicians interpret findings in medical images, for example by estimating whether a lesion on a [Mammograms](mammogram.md) is likely to be benign or malignant.

It's the step after [Computer-Aided Detection (CADe)](computer-aided-detection-cade.md): CADe locates suspicious regions, and CADx characterises them. The output is a second opinion for the clinician, who makes the final call.
