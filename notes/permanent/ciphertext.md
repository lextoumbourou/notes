**Ciphertext** is the encrypted form of a message: the output of running [Plaintext](plaintext.md) through an [Encryption](encryption.md) algorithm with a key.

Without the right key, ciphertext should look like meaningless data. With the key, it can be turned back into plaintext through [Decryption](decryption.md). See [Cryptography](cryptography.md).
