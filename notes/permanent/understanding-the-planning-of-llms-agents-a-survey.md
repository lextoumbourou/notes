Notes from paper [Understanding the planning of LLM agents: A survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02716) by Xu Huang, Weiwen Liu, Xiaolong Chen, Xingmei Wang, Hao Wang, Defu Lian, Yasheng Wang, Ruiming Tang, Enhong Chen

## Overview

A survey that explores the planning capabilities of [Large Language Models](large-language-models.md)s used as [AI Agents](ai-agents.md).

The authors categorise existing research into five directions:
* Task Decomposition
* Multi-Plan Selection
* External Planner-Aided Planning
* Reflection and Refinement
* Memory-augmented Planning

Each section discusses the motivation, key approaches, and limitations of the respective direction.

The survey also presents a detailed analysis of LLM-based agents' planning abilities, highlighting challenges like:
* [Hallucinations](hallucinations.md)
* Feasibility of Plans
* Efficiency of Plans
* Multi-Modal Environmental Feedback
