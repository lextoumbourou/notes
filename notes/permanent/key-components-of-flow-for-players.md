---

Schell says that the key components that promote [Flow State](flow-state.md) for players are:

* clear goals
* limited distractions
* the "hands and minds" are engaged
* direct and immediate feedback
* continuous challenge - ensuring that the game challenge beween "boredom and frustration"

References:

* [The Art of Game Design - A Book Of Lenses (2nd Edition) by Jesse Schell](../reference/books/the-art-of-game-design-a-book-of-lenses-2nd-edition.md) (Chapter 10. The Experience Is in the Player's Mind)
