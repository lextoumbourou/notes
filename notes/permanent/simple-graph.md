**Simple Graph** is a [Graphs](graphs-discrete-mathematics.md) with no loops (edges from a vertex to itself) and no parallel edges (more than one edge between the same pair of vertices).

In a simple graph with n vertices, each [Vertex](graph-vertex.md) can have a degree of at most n-1, since there are only n-1 other vertices it can connect to. Any more connections would need a loop or a parallel edge.

A [Complete Graph](complete-graph.md) is a simple graph where every pair of vertices is connected. See [Week 13 - Graphs A](../reference/moocs/coursera/uol-discrete-mathematics/week-13-graphs-a.md).
