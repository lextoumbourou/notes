**Improving the efficiency and reliability of digital time-stamping** is a 1993 paper by Dave Bayer, Stuart Haber and W. Scott Stornetta.

It follows on from [How to time-stamp a digital document](how-to-time-stamp-a-digital-document.md). One of its key ideas is grouping documents into a [Merkle Tree](merkle-tree.md), so that many documents can be time-stamped at once by publishing a single root hash.

It's one of the time-stamping papers referenced in [Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System](bitcoin-a-peer-to-peer-electronic-cash-system.md).
