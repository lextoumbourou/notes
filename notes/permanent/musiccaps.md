**MusicCaps** is a dataset of around 5.5k music clips, each paired with a rich text description written by musicians. The 10-second clips come from AudioSet.

It was released alongside [MusicLM](musiclm.md) in the paper [MusicLM: Generating Music From Text](../reference/papers/paper-musiclm-generating-music-from-text.md), as an evaluation set for text-to-music generation.
