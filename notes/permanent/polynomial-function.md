**Polynomial Function** is a function defined by a [Polynomial](polynomial.md), which has the form:

$f(x) = a_nx^n + a_{n-1}x^{n-1} + \dots + a_1x + a_0$

where the coefficients $a_i$ are constants and $n$ is a non-negative integer. The highest power with a non-zero coefficient is the degree of the polynomial.

For example, linear functions have degree 1, quadratic functions degree 2 and cubic functions degree 3.
