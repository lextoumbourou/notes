An open platform for agents that write code, use a command line and browse the web in sandboxed environments.

Source: [OpenHands: An Open Platform for AI Software Developers as Generalist Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16741v3).

Formerly OpenDevin. The preprint first appeared in 2024, and the paper was accepted at ICLR 2025.

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
