**Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)** is a medical imaging technique that uses strong magnetic fields and radio waves to produce detailed images of the soft tissue inside the body. Unlike X-rays or CT scans, it doesn't use ionising radiation.

In breast cancer detection, breast MRI is more sensitive than [Mammography](mammography.md), so it's used to screen people at high risk and to assess the extent of a known cancer. However, it's more expensive and slower, so it isn't used for routine screening.

See also [Ultrasound Imaging](ultrasound-imaging.md) and [Positron Emission Tomography (PET)](positron-emission-tomography-pet.md).
