In physics, velocity is a vector quantity that represents the rate of change of an object's position over time. It describes both the speed and direction of motion.

```python
change_in_position = final_position - initial_position
velocity = change_in_position / time_interval
```

Velocity is closely related to [Displacement](displacement.md) and Time. The relationship between these quantities is expressed in the fundamental equation of kinematics:

$\text{Velocity} = \frac{\text{Displacement}}{\text{Time}}$

This equation shows that velocity is the rate at which an object's position changes with respect to time. It's important to note that velocity is a vector quantity, meaning it has both magnitude (speed) and direction.

The concept of velocity is crucial in understanding Motion and forms the basis for more complex concepts like [Acceleration](acceleration.md) and Momentum. It plays a significant role in various fields of physics and engineering, from classical mechanics to relativity theory.
