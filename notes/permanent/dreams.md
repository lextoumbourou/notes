**Dreaming** is [OpenClaw](openclaw.md)'s scheduled background memory consolidation process. Its `memory-core` plugin reviews recent notes and eligible session transcripts, stages promising information, reflects on recurring themes, then promotes selected memories into `MEMORY.md` [@openclawDreaming].

It also writes a **Dream Diary** to `DREAMS.md`. The “Dreams” tab shows the diary and the process's status. The diary is for human review; it is not the agent's durable memory or a source for promoting new memories [@openclawDreaming].

Source: [OpenClaw's Dreaming documentation](https://docs.openclaw.ai/concepts/dreaming).
