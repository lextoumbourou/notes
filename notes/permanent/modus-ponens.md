**Modus ponens** is a rule of inference in [Propositional Logic](propositional-logic.md), which says that if $p \rightarrow q$ is true and $p$ is true, then $q$ must also be true:

$p \rightarrow q$
$p$
$\therefore q$

For example: "If it's raining, the ground is wet. It's raining. Therefore, the ground is wet."

It's based on [Implication](logical-implication.md), and is one of the simplest forms of a [Valid Argument](valid-argument.md).
