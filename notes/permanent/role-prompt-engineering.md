A **Role** in [Prompt Engineering](prompt-engineering.md) is an identity you ask the model to take on, like "You are an expert historian", to shape its response style and the scope of information it uses. It's often set in the [System Prompt](system-prompt.md).

See [Persona](persona-prompt-engineering.md).
