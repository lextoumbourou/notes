**Descriptive Statistics** is the part of statistics concerned with summarising and describing the main features of a dataset, as opposed to inferential statistics, which uses a sample to draw conclusions about a wider population.

Common tools include:

- Measures of central tendency, like the mean and [Median](median.md).
- Measures of spread, like [Variance](variance.md) and [Standard Deviation](standard-deviation.md).
- [Data Visualisation](data-visualisation.md), like a [Box Plot](box-plot.md).
