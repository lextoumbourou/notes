**Breast Cancer Detection** is the task of finding signs of breast cancer, like masses, calcifications and architectural distortions, in medical images of the breast, and classifying them as benign or malignant.

The most common imaging method for screening is [Mammography](mammography.md), but others include [Digital Breast Tomosynthesis (DBT)](digital-breast-tomosynthesis-dbt.md), [Ultrasound Imaging](ultrasound-imaging.md) and [Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)](magnetic-resonance-imaging-mri.md). Deep learning models, especially [CNN](convolutional-neural-network.md), are widely used to build [Computer-Aided Detection (CADe)](computer-aided-detection-cade.md) and [Computer-Aided Diagnosis (CADx)](computer-aided-diagnosis.md) systems that assist radiologists.

See [Wang, L. (2024) - Mammography with Deep Learning for Breast Cancer Detection](../reference/papers/3070/mammography-with-deep-learning-for-breast-cancer-detection.md) and the [CBIS-DDSM Mammography Dataset](cbis-ddsm-mammography-dataset.md).
