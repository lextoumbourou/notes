**Satoshi Nakamoto** is the pseudonymous creator of [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md). Satoshi published the whitepaper [Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System](bitcoin-a-peer-to-peer-electronic-cash-system.md) in 2008, released the first Bitcoin software in early 2009, then stepped away from the project around 2010 to 2011. Their real identity is still technically unknown, though [Adam Back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Back) has been found to be the likely candidate in a number of investigations.
