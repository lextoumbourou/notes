The **L1 Norm** of a vector is the sum of the absolute values of its components: $\|x\|_1 = \sum_i |x_i|$.

The L1 norm of the difference between two points is the absolute distance between them in a grid-like system. Also known as [Manhattan Distance](manhattan-distance.md).

See also [L2 Norm](l2-norm.md).
