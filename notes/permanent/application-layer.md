The **Application Layer** is the top layer of the [TCP/IP Five-Layer Network Model](tcp-ip-5-layer-network-model.md). It's where applications and the protocols they use to talk to each other live, such as [HTTP](http.md) for the web, SMTP for email and DNS for looking up domain names.

It relies on the [Transport Layer](transport-layer.md) below it to deliver data between hosts. In the [OSI Model](osi-model.md), this role is split across the application, presentation and session layers.
