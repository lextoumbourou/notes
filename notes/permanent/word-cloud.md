**Word Cloud** is a [Data Visualisation](data-visualisation.md) of text, where each word is displayed with a size proportional to how often it appears.

It gives a quick visual summary of the most common words in a document or corpus, usually after removing stop words. It's not great for precise comparisons, since it's hard to judge small differences in word size.
