**ACON** (Agent Context Optimization) refines compression guidelines using analysis of agent failures. Its guideline optimisation does not require fine-tuning the agent; the paper also distils the optimised compressor into smaller models.

Source: [ACON: Optimizing Context Compression for Long-horizon LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00615v3).

Referenced in [An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](an-empirical-study-of-harness-design-for-coding-agents.md).
