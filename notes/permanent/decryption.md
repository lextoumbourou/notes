**Decryption** is the process of turning [Ciphertext](ciphertext.md) back into readable [Plaintext](plaintext.md) using a key. It's the reverse of [Encryption](encryption.md).

In [Symmetric Cryptography](symmetric-cryptography.md), the same secret key is used to encrypt and decrypt. In [Asymmetric Cryptography](asymmetric-cryptography.md), a message encrypted with someone's public key can only be decrypted with their matching private key.
