**Fine-Tuning** is the act of continuing to train a pre-trained model on a new dataset, for a different problem or to improve performance. It's a key part of the process of [Transfer Learning](transfer-learning.md).

In the [LLM Training Pipeline](llm-training-pipeline.md), post-training techniques include [Supervised Fine-Tuning](supervised-fine-tuning.md) and preference tuning [@raschkaBuildReasoningModel2026].
