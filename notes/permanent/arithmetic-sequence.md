An **Arithmetic Sequence** (or arithmetic progression) is a [Sequence](sequence.md) where you get each term by adding a fixed amount to the previous term.

The starting value is the [First Term](first-term.md) ($), and the fixed amount is the [Common Difference](common-difference.md) ($). So the  term is:

 = a + (n - 1)d$

For example, start at 1 and add 6 each time: 1, 7, 13, 19, ... Here, 1 is the first term and 6 is the common difference.

Compare with [Geometric Progressions](geometric-progressions.md), where you multiply by a fixed amount instead.
