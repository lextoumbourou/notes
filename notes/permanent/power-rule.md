The **Power Rule** is a rule in calculus for finding the [Derivative](derivative.md) of $x$ raised to a constant power:

$$
\frac{d}{dx} x^n = n x^{n-1}
$$

For example, $\frac{d}{dx} x^2 = 2x$ and $\frac{d}{dx} x^3 = 3x^2$. It's one of the rules that makes derivatives quick to work out by hand, like when calculating the gradient of a squared error loss in [Gradient Descent](gradient-descent.md).

Logarithms have a rule with the same name, $\log_b(M^p) = p \cdot \log_b(M)$, which is covered in [Logarithm Properties](logarithm-properties.md).
