**Enhanced Voice Services** (EVS) is a voice compression standard. It's a speech codec standardised by 3GPP in 2014 for mobile voice calls, including Voice over LTE (VoLTE).

It's one of the traditional codecs that [Soundstream: An End-to-End Neural Audio Codec](soundstream-an-end-to-end-neural-audio-codec.md) compares against, along with [Opus Audio Codec](opus-audio-codec.md). See [Neural Audio Codec](neural-audio-codec.md).
