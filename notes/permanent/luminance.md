Luminance is a photometric measure of the [Brightness](brightness.md) of a surface or an area as perceived by the human eye, taking into account its intensity and the amount of light that passes through or is emitted from a particular area.

It is quantified in units of candela per square meter (cd/m²) and is used to describe how bright an object or light source appears to the human eye.
