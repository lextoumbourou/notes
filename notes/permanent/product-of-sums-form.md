**Product-of-Sums Form** is a standard way of writing a [Boolean Function](boolean-function.md), where terms built with the OR operator (sums) are combined with the AND operator (product).

For example: $f(x, y, z) = (x + y)(x + z)(y + z)$

It's the dual of the [Sum-of-Products Form](sum-of-products-form.md). To build it from a truth table, you focus on the rows where the function equals 0 instead of 1. See [Week 9 - Boolean Algebra A](../reference/moocs/coursera/uol-discrete-mathematics/week-9-boolean-algebra-a.md).
