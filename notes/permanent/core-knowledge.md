**Core Knowledge** is a theory from developmental psychology that humans are born with a small set of basic knowledge systems, which all later learning builds on.

In [On the Measure of Intelligence (2019)](../reference/papers/on-the-measure-of-intelligence-2019.md), Chollet uses Core Knowledge as the set of priors that the [Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus](abstraction-and-reasoning-corpus.md) (ARC) assumes, so humans and AI systems can be compared fairly. They include:

* Objectness and elementary physics
* Agentness and goal-directedness
* Natural numbers and elementary arithmetic
* Elementary geometry and topology
