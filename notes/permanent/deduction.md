**Deduction**, or deductive reasoning, is a type of [Logical Reasoning](logical-reasoning.md) where the conclusion must follow from the premises. If the argument is valid and all its premises are true, the conclusion is guaranteed to be true.

- Rule: All men are mortal.
- Observation: Socrates is a man.
- Conclusion: Socrates is mortal.

Compare this with [Induction](induction.md), which generalises from examples, and [Abduction](abduction.md), which finds a plausible explanation. Neither guarantees a true conclusion.
