**Semantic Content** is content that is marked up to describe what it means, rather than how it should look. For example, marking a piece of text as a `<title>` or an `<author>`, instead of just making it bold.

Because the meaning is explicit, machines can process and reuse the content, for example searching, transforming or restyling it. Markup languages like [XML](xml.md) are designed for this, and it's the core idea behind the [Semantic Web](semantic-web.md).
