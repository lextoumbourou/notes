# Surface brief: article page (and the theme around it)

## Scope and mode
Read. The article template (`theme/templates/article.html`) is the primary surface; home, lists and tags inherit its world. Reader: Lex first, wandering his own garden; secondary readers land on one note from search or social.

## Job
Read one note comfortably, then follow a link to a neighbour. Wayfinding: top bar (wordmark is home; nav Notes, Topics, About; user chose "Lede as heading (Recommended)" for home) with search, sidebar with On this page, Linked from and the graph.

## Constraints
Graph, backlinks and dark mode stay. Lex's words only: design never writes copy in his voice. Never em dashes. Marker face is Shantell Sans (user, shown a specimen against the comp: "B · Shantell Sans"; earlier: "A marker font is fine"). Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-a.png`, used as a reference (user chose "Reference" over "Pixel contract").

## Direction contract
THESIS: Lex's garden as a hand-drawn technical zine: the frame is lettered and sketched like a friend's napkin explanation, the column stays calm. Refuses the three-column docs app shell and the cream-card blog.
OWN-WORLD: Off-white paper #FBFAF6, marker ink #111, highlighter yellow #FFE84D, pink #FF6FAE, teal #2DD4BF (deepened for text). Shantell Sans for headings, wordmark, scribbles; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body; Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono code. Sketched rough.js outlines on code, search, graph nodes; marker underline swipes; highlighter `==marks==`. No cards-in-cards, no pills-in-cream. Dark: chalk on slate.
STORY: The reader sees the note immediately, understands it, notices Lex's hand in the margins, then follows a sketched link to a neighbour.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (wordmark left, nav, sketched search right, marker rule). Marker title with teal swipe, byline, tags, opening paragraph in a 68ch column; right sidebar: Linked from, hand-drawn graph with current note in pink.
